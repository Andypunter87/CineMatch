🍿 CineMatch Brand Guide

Last updated: April 2025

⸻

🧭 Brand Essence

CineMatch helps people find the right movie for right now — based on how they’re feeling and who they’re watching with. We reduce decision fatigue, spark discovery, and help people stop scrolling and start watching.

⸻

🗣️ Voice & Tone

Warm, smart, playful — emotionally intuitive.
	•	Warm & Helpful – Friendly, calm, approachable
	•	Smart, Not Snobby – Knows film, but never pretentious
	•	Playfully Clear – Punchy, casual, confident
	•	Emotion-Led – Leans on vibe, feeling, and mood over plot or genre

Examples:
	•	“Feeling nostalgic? Let’s cue up a classic.”
	•	“You rate, we recommend. Easy.”
	•	“No more endless scrolling. We’ve got you.”

⸻

🎨 Visual Identity

Colours
	•	Primary Blue: #4DA8F2
	•	Gradient Accent: Blue → Turquoise (#4DA8F2 → #64D9D9)
	•	Navy Text: #1C1C2B
	•	Muted Grey Text: #707070
	•	Background White: #FFFFFF

Typography
	•	Sans Serif (Inter, Helvetica Neue, or system default)
	•	Headers: Bold, Title Case
	•	Body: Clean, regular weight, dark blue

Style
	•	Rounded UI, clean white space
	•	Grid layout for film posters
	•	Simple, line-style icons (film, mood, popcorn etc.)

⸻

⭐ Core Features to Highlight
	•	Mood-first recommendations
	•	Hidden gem discovery
	•	Group-friendly suggestions
	•	Personalised watchlist
	•	Works across all major streaming services

⸻

📣 Messaging Pillars

Theme	Message Style
Mood Matching	“Tell us how you feel. We’ll find the film.”
Discovery	“Missed it? Not anymore.”
Group Picks	“Something everyone can agree on.”
Time-saving	“Stop scrolling. Start watching.”



⸻

🖼️ Asset Guidelines
	•	Onboarding slides: Minimal, fun, informative (use app style)
	•	Banners/Social Tiles: Mood + emoji + action (“In a thriller mood?” → try…)
	•	Icons/Illustrations: Match app style — minimal, rounded, blue-accented

⸻

✅ Copy Checklist for Any New Asset
	•	Is it emotionally intuitive?
	•	Does it sound like a friend who knows movies?
	•	Is it short and clear — no waffle?
	•	Does it help the user take action or discover something new?
