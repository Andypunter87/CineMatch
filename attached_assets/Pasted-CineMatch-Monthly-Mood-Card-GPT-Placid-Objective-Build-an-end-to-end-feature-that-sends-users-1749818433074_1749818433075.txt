CineMatch Monthly Mood Card (GPT + Placid)
🎯 Objective
Build an end-to-end feature that sends users a monthly “Mood Card” based on the films they matched with on CineMatch — complete with a custom vibe name generated by OpenAI and a beautiful, shareable visual created using Placid.app.

This Mood Card will be:

Delivered via email

Viewable and sharable from within the app

A monthly delight and growth loop

💡 Concept
Inspired by Spotify Daylist, this feature uses OpenAI to emotionally style a user’s monthly watchlist and give it a name like:

“Bittersweet & Beautiful”
“Sad Girls and Soft Light”
“French Existential Chaos”
“Films That Feel Like Sunday”

The mood is then turned into a beautiful Placid card for users to share on Instagram/X/etc.

✅ Functional Flow
1. 📊 Collect Monthly Film Data
At the start of each month:

For each active user, collect their Top 5 matched films from the previous month (by frequency, ratings, or another simple scoring model).

Example:

json
Copy
Edit
[
  "Lady Bird",
  "The Royal Tenenbaums",
  "Frances Ha",
  "Aftersun",
  "Eighth Grade"
]
2. 🧠 Generate Mood via OpenAI API
Use OpenAI’s GPT to analyse the list and return:

A poetic mood name (3–5 words max)

A short subtitle (tone: warm, stylish, slightly whimsical)

A background colour (hex code)

Optionally: a recommended emoji or image style

Prompt to use:
ts
Copy
Edit
await openai.chat.completions.create({
  model: "gpt-4",
  messages: [
    {
      role: "system",
      content: `
You are a poetic brand writer for CineMatch — a mood-based film recommendation service with a warm, stylish tone.

Your job is to analyse the vibe of a user's top 5 films and return:
- A one-line mood name (like a playlist title)
- A short subtitle that evokes feeling or aesthetic
- A suggested colour (hex code)
- Optionally: a matching emoji set

Tone: poetic, charming, cinematic, a bit nostalgic. Nothing generic or corporate. Always match the feel of the films.`
    },
    {
      role: "user",
      content: `The user's top 5 films this month are:
- The Royal Tenenbaums
- Frances Ha
- Lady Bird
- Aftersun
- Eighth Grade

Please return:
1. A poetic mood name
2. A subtitle (short phrase)
3. A background colour (hex)
4. 3 emojis`
    }
  ]
});

3. 🎨 Generate Card with Placid
Use the Placid API to generate a static social card.

Use a Placid template called monthly-mood-card with fields:

mood_name

subtitle

film_1 through film_5

bg_colour (solid or overlay)

poster_img (use the top film’s poster from TMDb)

CineMatch logo (static)

4. 📬 Email the User
Subject: Your Monthly CineMatch Mood is Ready
Preview: Bittersweet & Beautiful. Yes, that’s you.
Body:

Show teaser of the mood

Include 1–2 top films

Include CTA:

🎨 View Your Mood Card

CTA opens:
https://cinematch.app/mymood/2025-03?uid=1234

5. 📱 Landing Page (In App or Web)
Displays the share card (hosted via Placid)

Buttons:

📷 Share to Instagram Story

🐦 Share to X

💾 Download Image

📎 Copy Link

Bonus:

Button to “Get new matches with this vibe”

Option to view full monthly watchlist
